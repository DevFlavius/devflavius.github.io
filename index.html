01
About

      

        
I'm Flavio, a Brazilian developer living in Naples, Italy. I learned game programming in a technical high school (2016–2018), spent a few years working with my hands as an industrial mechanic and IT technician, and came back to code full-time in 2024.

        
That mix is my edge: I solve problems that cross software (APIs, scraping, databases, CRMs and ERPs) and hardware and networks (Linux servers, Docker, virtualization, IoT). If it can be automated or self-hosted, I want to automate it and self-host it.

        
I work AI-native: I direct coding agents like Claude Code, and I own what makes software right: the spec, the acceptance criteria and the risk calls.
